Who we are

About The Chronicle

A student-led publication committed to documenting our community with curiosity, fairness and respect.

Our mission

CBC The Chronicle exists to make room for student experiences, ideas and creative work. We aim to inform our community, encourage thoughtful inquiry, and create a space where different perspectives can be expressed and considered.

Our values

  • Diverse voices: We welcome contributions shaped by different experiences, interests and ways of seeing the world.
  • Respectful disagreement: We believe disagreement can strengthen understanding when people engage with ideas fairly and without personal attacks.
  • Student autonomy: Students should have meaningful opportunities to choose subjects, develop arguments and express their own views.
  • Accuracy and accountability: We verify factual claims where reasonably possible, identify uncertainty and correct significant errors.
  • Care and privacy: We consider the potential effects of publication, especially where minors or vulnerable people may be involved.
  • Editorial independence: Editorial decisions are guided by the publication's standards, not by pressure to promote a particular viewpoint.
  • Editorial review: Editors may check facts, request sources, suggest revisions, add context, or decline material. Substantial changes should be discussed with the author where practical.
  • Fair representation: We distinguish reporting from opinion, seek relevant perspectives and avoid presenting speculation as established fact.

Copyright and publication rights

Authors retain copyright in their original submissions. By submitting work, authors grant CBC The Chronicle a non-exclusive license to publish, display and archive accepted work on the newspaper's website and associated publication channels, with appropriate attribution. This license does not transfer ownership. Authors should identify third-party material and obtain permission where required. The newspaper will not knowingly claim ownership of student work.

Comment policy

Comments and reader responses are moderated before publication. We welcome specific, relevant criticism and disagreement. Editors may shorten or edit a response for clarity, remove identifying details, request revisions or decline publication. We do not publish comments automatically.

Speech boundaries

Open discussion does not mean every form of expression is suitable for publication. We will not publish content that threatens or encourages violence, targets people with harassment, promotes discrimination or dehumanization, exposes private information, makes unsupported damaging allegations about identifiable people, or creates a serious safety risk. Criticism of institutions, policies, public decisions and ideas is welcome when expressed responsibly.

What we do not accept

  • Plagiarism, fabricated quotations, manipulated evidence or knowingly false information.
  • Threats, targeted harassment, bullying, slurs or discriminatory attacks.
  • Private contact details, confidential records, or images of people shared without appropriate permission.
  • Unverified accusations about identifiable students, staff or community members.
  • Content that encourages dangerous conduct or puts a student at risk.
  • Advertising, impersonation, spam, or submissions that exist mainly to promote a product or private account.
  • Material that infringes copyright or other people's rights.

Editorial responsibility

Publication is a decision made by the editorial team. We may ask for clarification, sources, consent or revisions before publication. We may withhold or remove content when credible concerns arise. If you believe we have made an error, please use our Corrections page.
