A few useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can submit?

Students are welcome to submit work to any section. You do not need to be a professional writer or journalist. If you are unsure where your idea belongs, choose the closest category and explain it briefly.

Does every submission get published?

No. The editorial team reviews submissions for relevance, accuracy, originality, clarity, safety and fit with the publication's standards. Editors may suggest revisions or decline a piece.

Can I submit anonymously?

You may use an anonymous byline or leave the byline field blank. Editors may still need a private way to contact you for clarification. We will not publish your identity without your permission, except where disclosure is required for safety or legal reasons.

Do I keep the copyright to my work?

Yes. Student authors retain copyright in their original work. By submitting, you ask the newspaper to consider it for publication. If accepted, you grant CBC The Chronicle a non-exclusive license to publish and display the work on its website and associated publication channels, with attribution where requested. Any broader reuse should be discussed with the author.

Can I submit a link or work created by someone else?

Yes, in Academics & Ideas, if it is relevant and you provide the original link, author and publisher. Do not upload or reproduce someone else's work without permission. Short quotations should be clearly attributed and used for commentary or discussion.

How are comments handled?

Reader responses are sent to the editorial inbox and are not published automatically. Editors review them against the comment policy. Approved responses may be edited for clarity or length, without changing their meaning.

How do I report an error?

Use the Corrections page and include the article title, the relevant passage and a reliable source if possible.

Still curious?

Ask the editorial team

Questions are sent privately to the editorial inbox. Please do not include confidential or sensitive personal information.
